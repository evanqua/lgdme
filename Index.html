The Large DME (Durable Medical Equipment) System connects residents who are donating large durable medical equipment with residents who need it. There is no cost to participate, and no account is required to browse listings.

The matches donors and requesters of items such as hospital beds, wheelchairs, and stair lifts. To participate, submit a listing to donate or to request an item. When a match is found, both parties receive an email notification. No information is shared before a match occurs.

This is not a platform to buy or sell items, so all equipment must be exchanged free of charge. Matched donors and recipients may privately negotiate the cost of delivery or installation services between themselves.

How it works

  1. 1Submit a listingTell us what you're donating or looking for, and any other details you think might be helpful.
  2. 2Receive matchesWe will email you as soon as we find a matching listing. There is no need to make additional submissions.
  3. 3Arrange privatelyYou and your match coordinate pickup or drop-off directly. This organization does not inspect, or handle items exchanged in the system.
Please note: cannot provide donation receipts for items exchanged through this system. Because donated items go directly from the donor to the recipient, our organization never takes physical possession of, inspects, or otherwise verifies the item or the exchange. Under IRC §170(f)(8), a charity can only issue a valid contemporaneous written acknowledgment (the document required to substantiate a tax-deductible noncash contribution) for property it has actually received. Because we never receive or control the equipment, we are not in a position to make that attestation, and cannot issue a tax-deductible receipt for these donations. Donors who need a receipt for tax purposes should discuss options directly with the recipient or a qualified tax advisor.
