# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project exists to help organizations connect people who have durable medical equipment to give away with people who need it. Everyone interacting with this project, in an issue, a pull request, a discussion, or any other project space, is expected to treat other participants with basic courtesy and good faith.

In practice, this means:

- Disagree about code and decisions, not about people.
- Assume good intent, and give people the benefit of the doubt before assuming bad faith.
- Give and accept feedback in a way that is direct but respectful.
- Do not use this project's spaces for harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, or sexualized content.
- Respect that contributors here are largely volunteers giving their own time; a slow response is far more often a matter of limited time than a lack of interest.

## Scope

This applies to every space connected to this project: the repository itself (issues, pull requests, code review comments, discussions), and any other communication channel the maintainers designate as an official project space.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior in a project space falls short of this standard, report it privately to the project's maintainer rather than escalating publicly in the same thread. **PLACEHOLDER: the maintainer has not yet published a dedicated contact address for this; until one exists, use a private message through GitHub to a maintainer listed on the repository.**

Reports are handled in confidence to the extent possible. Retaliation against someone for making a good-faith report is itself a violation of this policy.

## Enforcement

A maintainer who receives a report will look into it and respond with whatever action fits the situation, which can range from a private conversation, to a public warning, to removal from the project's spaces for repeated or serious violations. There is no fixed formula; the goal is a project space that stays usable and welcoming for people trying to do good-faith work.

## Attribution

This document is a short, project-specific policy rather than an adoption of a longer standard template. If you are more comfortable with a fuller reference, the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) is a well-established starting point that projects like this one commonly adapt from.
